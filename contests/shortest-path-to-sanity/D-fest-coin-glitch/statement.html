Problem D

The Fest Coin Glitch

Difficulty: medium. Time limit: 2 seconds.

At the college fest, you pay with fest coins. The fest has n stalls, numbered 1 to n: stall 1 is the entry and stall n is the exit.

There are m one-way walkways. Walking from stall a to stall b along a walkway changes your coins by x: a game you win (x > 0) or a snack you can't resist (x < 0). You start at the entry with 0 coins and can go into debt. You may pass any stall, the exit included, as many times as you like, and you leave whenever you are standing at the exit.

What is the most coins you can leave with? If there is a way to leave with as many coins as you like, the organisers have a glitch, and you print JACKPOT.

Input

The first line contains two integers n and m (2 ≤ n ≤ 1000, 1 ≤ m ≤ 3000).

Each of the next m lines contains three integers a, b and x (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, −109 ≤ x ≤ 109). A walkway can lead from a stall back to itself.

The exit can always be reached from the entry.

Output

Print the most coins you can leave with, or JACKPOT.

Examples

Sample input 1

4 4
1 2 5
2 4 3
1 3 -2
3 4 20

Sample output 1

18

Explanation. 1 → 3 → 4 costs 2 coins and then wins 20: 18 coins. 1 → 2 → 4 gives only 8.

Sample input 2

4 4
1 2 1
2 3 2
3 2 -1
3 4 0

Sample output 2

JACKPOT

Explanation. Going 2 → 3 → 2 wins 2 and pays 1, so every round is one more coin. Go round as often as you like, then walk to the exit.

Sample input 3

3 3
1 3 -5
1 2 1
2 2 10

Sample output 3

-5

Explanation. Stall 2 has a +10 walkway to itself, but from stall 2 there is no way to the exit. The only way out costs 5 coins.
