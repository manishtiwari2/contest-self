Problem E

Renovation Season

Difficulty: hard. Time limit: 2 seconds.

The campus is being renovated, and the buildings reopen one at a time, in order of their numbers. The campus has n buildings, numbered 1 to n, and m roads. Road i joins buildings ai and bi in both directions and takes ti minutes.

On day x, buildings 1 to x are open (on day 0, none are). Roads only meet inside buildings, so every building you walk through on the way must be open. The building you start in and the building you go to are always fine: you're already inside one, and the other has a side door just for you.

Answer q questions: on day x, what is the fastest way from building a to building b?

Input

The first line contains three integers n, m and q (1 ≤ n ≤ 150, 0 ≤ m ≤ 2·104, 1 ≤ q ≤ 105).

Each of the next m lines contains three integers a, b and t (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, 1 ≤ t ≤ 109). There can be several roads between the same buildings.

Each of the next q lines contains three integers a, b and x (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, 0 ≤ x ≤ n).

Output

For each question, print on its own line the fastest time in minutes, or -1 if you can't get there that day. Going from a building to itself takes 0 minutes.

Examples

Sample input 1

4 4 6
1 2 4
2 3 4
1 3 10
3 4 3
1 3 0
1 3 2
1 4 2
1 4 3
4 4 0
4 2 3

Sample output 1

10
8
-1
11
0
7

Explanation. On day 0 only the direct road 1–3 works: 10 minutes. On day 2 you may walk through building 2, and 1 → 2 → 3 takes 8. Going from 1 to 4 needs building 3 in the middle, which opens on day 3; then 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 takes 11. From 4 to 4 you are already there. On day 3, 4 → 3 → 2 takes 7.

Sample input 2

3 1 2
1 2 7
1 3 3
2 1 0

Sample output 2

-1
7

Explanation. No road reaches building 3. The direct road between 2 and 1 doesn't pass through any building.
