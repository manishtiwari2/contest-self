Problem A

Gossip Express

Difficulty: easy. Time limit: 2 seconds.

Somebody saw the class topper crying over a B grade, and the news is spreading faster than hostel Wi-Fi ever has.

The class has n students, numbered 1 to n, and m friendships. Friendship works both ways. At minute 0, k students already know the gossip. Every minute, everyone who knows it tells all their friends, and those friends know it from the next minute on.

For every student, find the minute they first hear it.

Input

The first line contains three integers n, m and k (1 ≤ n ≤ 2·105, 0 ≤ m ≤ 2·105, 1 ≤ k ≤ n).

The second line contains k different integers: the students who know the gossip at minute 0.

Each of the next m lines contains two integers a and b (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, a ≠ b): a and b are friends. The same friendship can appear more than once.

Output

Print n integers separated by spaces: for each student 1 to n, the minute they first hear the gossip, or -1 if they never do. (Lucky them.)

Examples

Sample input 1

6 4 1
1
1 2
1 3
2 4
4 5

Sample output 1

0 1 1 2 3 -1

Explanation. Student 1 tells students 2 and 3 in minute 1, student 2 tells student 4 in minute 2, and student 4 tells student 5 in minute 3. Student 6 has no friends in this class, so never hears it.

Sample input 2

5 4 2
1 5
1 2
2 3
3 4
4 5

Sample output 2

0 1 2 1 0

Explanation. The gossip starts from both ends at once, and student 3 hears it from both sides in minute 2.
