Problem C

All Roads Lead to the Canteen

Difficulty: medium. Time limit: 2 seconds.

Samosas come out of the fryer at 5 PM sharp, and they are gone by 5:05. You and your friends each want to get there as fast as possible, and nobody wants to run the same way as anyone else.

The campus has n spots, numbered 1 to n: spot 1 is your hostel and spot n is the canteen. There are m roads. Road i joins spots ai and bi in both directions and takes ti minutes.

Find the fastest time from the hostel to the canteen, and how many different fastest routes there are. Two routes are different if they use a different sequence of roads. Two roads between the same two spots are different roads.

Input

The first line contains two integers n and m (2 ≤ n ≤ 105, 1 ≤ m ≤ 2·105).

Each of the next m lines contains three integers a, b and t (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, 1 ≤ t ≤ 109). A road can join a spot to itself.

Output

Print two integers: the fastest time, and the number of fastest routes modulo 109 + 7. If the canteen can't be reached, print -1 0.

Examples

Sample input 1

4 5
1 2 3
2 4 4
1 3 5
3 4 2
1 4 8

Sample output 1

7 2

Explanation. 1 → 2 → 4 and 1 → 3 → 4 both take 7 minutes. The direct road takes 8.

Sample input 2

3 3
1 2 5
2 1 5
2 3 1

Sample output 2

6 2

Explanation. There are two different roads between spots 1 and 2, so there are two fastest routes, each 6 minutes long.

Sample input 3

3 1
1 2 4

Sample output 3

-1 0

Explanation. No road reaches spot 3.
