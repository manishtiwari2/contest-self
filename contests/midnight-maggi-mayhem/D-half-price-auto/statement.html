Problem D

Half-Price Auto

Difficulty: hard. Time limit: 2 seconds.

Your train back from home gets in at 2 AM, and your wallet is nearly empty. The city has n auto stops, numbered 1 to n: stop 1 is the railway station and stop n is your hostel gate.

There are m auto routes. Route i goes one way from stop ai to stop bi and costs ci rupees. (Going back the other way is a different route, if it exists at all.)

A friendly auto driver gave you one coupon: use it on any single ride to pay half its fare, rounded down. What is the cheapest way to get from the station to the hostel?

Input

The first line contains two integers n and m (2 ≤ n ≤ 105, 1 ≤ m ≤ 2·105).

Each of the next m lines contains three integers a, b and c (1 ≤ a, b ≤ n, 1 ≤ c ≤ 109): a one-way ride from a to b costing c rupees. There can be several routes between the same stops, and routes from a stop back to itself.

Output

Print the minimum total cost, or -1 if the hostel can't be reached. (Time to call a friend.)

Examples

Sample input 1

4 5
1 2 10
2 4 40
1 3 30
3 4 10
2 3 5

Sample output 1

20

Explanation. Ride 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 for 10 + 5 + 10 = 25 rupees, and use the coupon on one of the 10-rupee rides: 20 rupees. Using it on the 40-rupee ride of 1 → 2 → 4 still costs 10 + 20 = 30.

Sample input 2

3 2
1 2 7
3 2 4

Sample output 2

-1

Explanation. The only route touching stop 3 goes from 3 to 2, not the other way, so the hostel can't be reached.

Sample input 3

2 1
1 2 9

Sample output 3

4

Explanation. The single ride costs 9 rupees, and half of 9 rounded down is 4.
